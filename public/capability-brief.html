How it works

Onboarded once. Sharpened forever.

Day one

Onboarding loads everything.

Your first session is the foundation. The system learns the way you work, the way you write, the way you think, and the way you decide. By the time onboarding is done, your COB knows you. Your standards are loaded. Your voice is set. Your context is captured.

You start with a system that already understands who you are and what you care about.

Decade ten

Intelligence compounds forever.

Every conversation adds to the picture. Every decision sticks. Every preference is remembered. After a year, it speaks your voice fluently. After ten years, it carries a depth of context no new hire could ever build.

It travels with you, no matter where your work goes. The intelligence is portable, permanent, and entirely yours.
