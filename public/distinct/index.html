Protect . security first

AI is not Google. What you put in can come back on you.

Search takes information out. AI puts your information in. On a free tool, what you paste can be retained, used to train the model, and, the part no one says out loud, it becomes a discoverable record. In a dispute it can be subpoenaed like email. Treat every prompt like something a lawyer could read aloud.
