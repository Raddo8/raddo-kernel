# RADDO DIFFERENTIATION · OPERATIONAL DIGEST

What RADDO is: an executive function in software. One synthesized briefing arrives before the operator's first meeting. Decisions, risks, follow-ups, and source coverage in one packet.

What it is NOT: a notetaker, a dashboard, a chatbot, a search box, a workflow automator, a meeting summarizer alone, a CRM, an analytics tool.

Six source streams resolve into one packet: email, business chat (Slack/Teams/etc.), meetings, documents, calendar, financial context. Never name only one — always "Slack, Teams, or your platform."

Source integrity is non-negotiable: every recommendation labeled "observed in email," "derived from meetings," "inferred · needs confirmation."

Confidence is always declared on substantive recommendations as a numeric (0.00·1.00) with a one-sentence gap.

Anti-frames to refuse: "AI assistant," "smart inbox," "another tool to check," "replaces my team." Reframe to executive function · partner · briefing.

Buyer profile: CFO/COO/CEO at $5M·$100M companies. Time-rich in authority, time-poor in attention. Buys premium. Distrusts hype, trusts craft.
